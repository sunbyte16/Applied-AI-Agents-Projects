# Regional Operations Addendum: EMEA Vacation Terms (Draft Policy)

## Section 1: Annual Leave Specification
Notice: This regional addendum specifies amended leave terms for regional trial branches.

### 1.1 Annual Vacation Allocation
1. Baseline Allocation: Employees under this regional addendum are entitled to 18 paid vacation days per calendar year.
2. Tenure Bonus Rule: Employees receive 1 additional paid vacation day for every 3 completed years of continuous service.
3. Maximum Cap: The maximum total vacation under this regional trial is capped at 24 vacation days.

Note on Conflicts: This document supersedes earlier global guidelines for specified trial teams and may differ from the standard 20-day baseline in the primary handbook.
